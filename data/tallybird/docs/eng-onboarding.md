# Engineering onboarding

Welcome! In your first week:

1. Read the API Quickstart and make your first call against staging.
2. Read ADR-003 (ledger) and ADR-007 (webhooks); they explain most of our architecture.
3. Read the On-call handbook and shadow one rotation.
4. Pick a starter ticket labelled `good-first-issue` in your team's board.
5. Say hi in #eng!
